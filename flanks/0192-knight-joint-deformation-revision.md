The source L0 sleeve is a continuous, smoothly shaded tube, originally authored for a rigid arm. Its round section and gradual taper provide little shape to identify the elbow. The current standing/ready deformation blends the elbow transforms over 0.148569 m and introduces the wrist transform over 0.196504 m. Ready also blends thigh/shin transforms over 0.263900 m and shin/foot over 0.200200 m. These broad transitions can distribute curvature into the limb segments and soften the joint. L1 uses the same transforms, but its simpler angular surface makes the elbow more readable. The screenshot supports a combined surface/deformation problem. The relative contribution of geometry, weights and smooth shading must be isolated during the revision; passing Jacobian and joint-position checks did not establish a natural surface.

Compatibility assessment before authoring: the current animation paths use rest positions, part IDs and pivot/joint coordinates rather than a fixed vertex ordering. The shipping knight uses pivot-derived procedural sword motion; the saved diagonal slash and shuffle are separate asset-side clips. The generic jointed-attack loader has a 1 mm joint check, but that is not the knight slash playback path. Keeping those coordinates, limb lengths, IDs, weapon geometry and grip preserves structural compatibility and the existing animation data. New vertex geometry still needs motion and intersection checks. A mesh-only update cannot change the broad standing/ready blend formulas; the separate candidate will include revised deformation rules for these asset-side poses. No existing attack table or shared engine shader will be edited. Approval of the original poses remains valid, and integration remains deferred until the basic knight set is complete.
