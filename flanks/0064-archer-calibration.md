# 0064: archer damage calibration from range logs

Date: 2026-07-29. Owner call: settle BASE_DMG / range / ammo with data before the archer branch merges. Method: the Archery scenario as a fixed range (two 500-bow regiments vs a static 500-man block at ~110 m), FL_ARCHERY_TARGET=heavy|spear swaps the target's armour class, kills read from the [archery] log at t=52 (~4630 arrows loosed).

## The problem, measured

With the melee damage curve (BASE 40 x 1.13^factor), kills per loosed arrow:

- shielded lights (armour 3 + shield 4, frontal): 9.4%
- plate heavies (armour 5 + shield 5, frontal): 3.7%

The M2TW anchor (Withwnar's controlled tests, devlog 0060) is ~10% of arrows killing vs UNARMORED peasants. We were AT the unarmored anchor against shielded troops, and armour only bought a 2.5x spread. One archer regiment's full quiver (15,000 arrows) annihilated ~2.8 light regiments. That was the OP.

## The fix: missiles get their own factor curve

`missile::FACTOR_MULT = 1.28` (vs melee's 1.13), `missile::BASE_DMG = 20`. Armour classes now separate hard under arrows, which IS the M2TW missile feel (bodkin AP exists precisely because plain arrows bounce off plate). Same factor still drives the stagger roll, unchanged.

Measured after (same protocol):

- shielded lights: 4.4% kills/arrow (204 kills / 4631 arrows) — full quiver ~= 1.3 regiments, strong but no longer an auto-delete
- heavies: ZERO frontal kills in the 1-minute exchange (7.4 dmg/hit vs 160 hp = ~22 accumulated hits to kill; chip damage builds, kills only arrive under sustained concentration) — deliberate: plain arrows vs plate is the matchup AP bodkins are for; heavy REAR arcs still bite (factor +1, ~6 hits)
- unarmored proxy (shield bypassed, factor +3): ~2.1 hits/kill -> ~11%/arrow at the measured ~23% body-hit rate, right on the Withwnar anchor
- archer-vs-archer (factor +5): 1-2 hits/kill — counter-archery duels are lethal, as in M2TW

## Range and ammo: unchanged, deliberately

120 m and 30 arrows are M2TW-verbatim (devlog 0060). Time-under-fire closing the gap is ~13 s vs M2TW's ~18 (our troops run faster); the earlier plan's note stands — if approach feels too safe, stretch range toward 150 rather than slowing troops. Ammo at the ~9.5 s cycle = ~5 minutes of sustained fire.
