## Accepted shrub B and technique summary

Gota accepted the fuller v4 appearance and requested that the asset and progress be committed. The active shrub is assets/vegetation/shrub_b_v4.glb, sourced from assets_dev/vegetation/shrub_b_v4_fuller. The first rounded shrub, preferred open shrub and middle-density version remain available as separate shelf assets. The dense-atlas experiment remains in the private asset repository. The accepted v4 close mesh has 1,854 triangles at 0.8 m high, 2.5 m wide and 1.85 m deep. Acceptance covers the close appearance; the inherited 120-triangle middle mesh and four-triangle far cards still need matching to the approved canopy.

The asset is built deterministically in headless Blender from Python, not from a generated 3D mesh. Five tapered woody stems follow hand-authored paths from the ground, with three smaller forks. Capped polygonal tubes provide solid bark surfaces (222 triangles), using opaque bark and continuous mirrored UV coordinates to avoid exposed seams. The branch paths determine where foliage grows.

Built-in imagegen supplied an original transparent RGBA atlas of four fine hawthorn-like shoots. Each cell has a thin stem and small lobed leaves; source pixels and provenance remain on disk with the script. Blender scales the leaf image to 1024 square. A shoot's UVs map its basal stem to the branch attachment and its tip along the growth direction. The foliage uses double-sided alpha masking, so transparent gaps come from texture alpha rather than blended transparency. The atlas is reused unchanged through the open, middle and fuller versions. No photographic reference pixels ship in the asset.

The approved form contains 704 textured sprays: 112 shallow folded sprays (four triangles each) and 592 simple planes (two triangles each), totaling 1,632 foliage triangles. Varied direction, roll, length and width make the sprays overlap in three dimensions along the actual branch paths. Dedicated fixed random seeds for later layers preserve the original geometry instead of reshuffling the whole bush. Smooth custom normals combine the shoot plane with an upward bias and a small fold contribution. Linear leaf tint stays (0.66, 0.73, 0.72), with per-spray variation from 0.91 to 1.05.

The first revision kept 224 shoots and an open branch structure. The middle pass added 96 small inner shoots. The accepted fuller pass added 384 more across 24–91% of each branch path. Individual added sprays shorten uniformly when necessary to fit the original bounds; this preserves leaf proportions and avoids clipping cards to a box. Low woody bases and the outermost tips retain openings. This produced density by layering fine leaves, rather than enlarging leaves or replacing them with broad dense texture patches. The broad dense-atlas experiment looked like an oversized weed to Gota and was not used.

Export uses identity transforms, metres, +Y up, nodes L0/L1/card under variant_0, UV0 for texture coordinates, UV1 for bend/foliage markers and RGBA vertex colours with alpha one. The raw GLB checks verify dimensions, triangle budgets, finite attributes, material modes, closed bark winding and retained geometry. All v3 triangles and textures survive unchanged in v4, with exactly 768 foliage triangles added. Build and strict all-target clippy pass; the rendering code is unchanged since those checks. Existing foliage-mip tests passed when the shrub rendering support was introduced. Scene captures and recorded camera sweeps checked the visible shape and level transitions; they are not populated-map performance measurements.

Rendering support includes height-scaled ground sink (1 cm for this shrub, retaining 8 cm for the trees), projected maximum mesh dimension for LOD selection so a wide low shrub keeps detail, and the existing camera sweep respecting FL_CAM_DIST for close inspection. Diff review shows rendering, camera and assets only: no simulation, heightfield, water, shared RNG or new FL switch change. Asset binaries use the existing vegetation GLB LFS rule.
